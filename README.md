Visits before 6/5/26 (Dyads 01-09) will have completed the old version of this task, where the response period was 10 seconds and the fixation period was 5 seconds.

After this date, the response period is 3 seconds, and the fixation period is 15 seconds plus the remainder of the 3 seconds after the participant responded. There is also a 15 second fixation between "runs."
